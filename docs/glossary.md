# Glossary

## ATR

Average True Range, used as a volatility reference.

## Base

One or more compact candles between the leg-in and leg-out.

## Demand zone

A price area created by a bullish departure from a qualified base.

## Distal line

The zone boundary farther from the current market approach direction.

## ERC

An expansion candle that passes both the body-to-range and range-to-ATR requirements.

## Leg-In

The candle immediately preceding the base in formation order.

## Leg-Out

The directional departure candle that initiates zone detection.

## Proximal line

The zone boundary closer to the expected market approach direction.

## Supply zone

A price area created by a bearish departure from a qualified base.
