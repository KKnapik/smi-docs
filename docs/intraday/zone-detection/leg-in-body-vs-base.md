# Leg-In Body vs Base

**Default:** `1.50`

## Terms used on this page

- **Leg-In** — the move that approaches the future zone area.
- **Base** — the short pause before the departure.
- **Body** — the solid part of a candle between its open and close.

## What this setting controls

It controls how clearly the approach candle must stand out from the candles inside the base. At the default value of `1.50`, the Leg-In body must be at least one and a half times the size of the largest body in the base.

## If you lower the value

- Weaker approaches can qualify.
- More re-accumulation and re-distribution formations may appear.
- The transition from approach to pause can be less obvious.

## If you raise the value

- The Leg-In must look more distinct from the base.
- Fewer formations qualify.
- Gradual approaches followed by a valid departure may be rejected.

## Visual for this setting

The largest candle body inside the base will be used as a visual reference. One Leg-In will exceed the selected multiple, while another will remain too small.
